# Useless-Angel-Rina

## Overview

A multiple route visual novel inspired by the otome genre, following the troubled and troubling protagonist Rina as her misguided desires drop her into greater and greater danger. 

Rina's fate will change drastically depending on the player's choices, consider this game as an escape into a more fantastical reality as you either guide Rina to a happier life or drag her deeper into Hell!

## Features

There are four romantic options, three Hellish and one Heavenly! You will be tasked with guiding Rina through the branching plot, either helping her build up her assertiveness or enabling her failing mental health to lead her towards some kind of ending. 

Regardless of who you pick, it can be a struggle to true happiness or catastrophic doom, so feel free to experiment! 

Consider this visual novel a dark comedy, one that'll make you laugh at the antics and cry with Rina when everything goes wrong. The reverse may also be true. 

## The Cast

Rina - Our protagonist, a two-winged cherub soldier who constantly finds herself in messy situations. A bit of a lonely and clingy hopeless romantic, she is primed to get into all kinds of chaos with the love interests. Her mental health will affect just how well she survives each romance route. Rina is haunted by some darkness in her past and her coping mechanisms have not allowed her to find a peaceful resolution just yet. These coping mechanisms can get much more dangerous with player input. 

Yamato - A dragon demon from the Kurona clan, the descendents of a human man cursed by Beelzebub. The youngest of the love interests, he is trying to learn how to be a 'proper' duke of Hell from his cousin Kuro, but his soft-hearted nature makes his life a little difficult. He is frequently pushed around by the other dukes. With Rina's support however, he wishes to become a stronger, more hardy individual. 

Kuro - The older Kurona duke with the heart of a beast and a no-nonsense approach to a life he has earned through stubbornness and a lack of pity. Despite being surrounded by loyal servants and mentoring Yamato, he is quite isolated - something that Rina might resonate with. Once he sets his mind on something, he will not let go. He is persistently in a turf war with his rival Amadeus in the Alter World. 

Amadeus - The son of Asmodeus, he is notorious for both his more amorous mannerisms and spontaneous acts of violence towards enemies and allies alike. If there's a power struggle going on involving the dukes of Hell and their hopeful successors, Amadeus is bound to be involved. He treats life and love like a game with a ruthless and pragmatic approach, dragging Rina into the fray with playful apathy. 

Raphael - Archangel and ultimate healer, Rina found herself under his care several centuries ago during the darkest chapter of her life. As such, he's the closest thing she has to a home. As a shapeshifting angel, he takes a visage most comforting to his conversation partner, however Rina's psyche perceives this as 'perpetually stressed'. In spite of this, he is soothing and forever patient, albeit rather romantically dense.
